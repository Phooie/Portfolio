SmartCare: Smart Healthcare Management

Mini Thesis | 2026

A deep learning-based healthcare management system that automates pill dispension, medication intake scheduling and alarm system, medicine interaction checking and manages pill compartments using hardware integration, emotion monitoring and behavioral reports, and pill intake detection. It uses Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and LSTMs for gesture/emotion capture and video frame analysis.
